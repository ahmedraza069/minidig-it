01

Initial Discussion

02

Site Assessment

We inspect access, existing surfaces, ground conditions, damp-proof course levels and surface water drainage requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide complete driveway and patio projects to the final finish?

Yes. We are complete driveway builders and patio installers—carrying out the entire project from initial excavation, waste removal, drainage and MOT Type 1 sub-base preparation right through to laying your finished block paving, gravel or patio slabs.

Why is sub-base preparation important?

A properly excavated, membrane-lined and mechanically compacted MOT Type 1 sub-base supports vehicle weight and prevents sinking, rutting or movement over time.

Can you remove an existing driveway or patio?

Yes, we break out and remove existing concrete, tarmac, old paving slabs and soil, including full muck-away and waste disposal as part of your quotation.
