FAQs

Common drainage questions.

Need quick advice? Call us on 07448 756 349 or message us directly on WhatsApp.

Do you handle complete drainage installations as well as blocked drains?

Yes. We are complete drainage installers handling new foul and storm drainage, crate soakaways, manholes, septic tanks and full surface reinstatement, alongside rapid blocked drain clearance and jetting.

Can you carry out CCTV drain surveys and Home Buyer reports?

Yes. We arrange high-definition CCTV camera inspections to diagnose recurring blockages, locate root ingress or cracked clay pipes, and provide pre-purchase drain surveys for home buyers.

Do you cover both no-dig drain relining and full excavation repairs?

Yes. Where a pipe can be restored without digging, we offer structural drain relining. Where pipes have collapsed or require new falls, our in-house team excavates, installs new uPVC pipework and reinstates your driveway or patio to a seamless finish.

Do you cover domestic and commercial drainage across Maidenhead?

Yes, we work with homeowners, landlords, builders, property developers and commercial businesses across Taplow, Maidenhead, Slough, Windsor, Marlow, Ascot and an 18-mile radius.
